Help

Support & questions

The fastest way to reach us is email — we read every message.

Email support

Found a bug or have a request? Email support@oqra.app with your iPhone model, iOS version, and a screenshot if you can.

Isn't this just a filter?

No. Oqra runs a film-physics engine — every shot is rebuilt from your full-resolution RAW through light-sensitive layers, with grain, halation, and dye. The look is part of the photo, not a filter laid on top.

Does Oqra copy classic film stocks?

No. Oqra models how film behaves and leaves the look to you. Presets aren't meant to be exact replicas. They're for playing and experimenting.

What's a preset, and how do I make or share one?

A preset is a look you load, then point and shoot. Build your own in the editor — set each layer's tone, dye, grain, and contrast — and save it to your collection. Every preset folds into a recipe: share it as a link or QR code, and anyone with Oqra can add it. Sent one to open? Tap their oqra.app/p/… link or scan the QR and it loads straight in.

Can I apply a preset to photos I've already taken, or change it later?

Yes to both. Open a preset in the editor, load a photo from your library or Files, and export it to develop and save a copy. Shots you take in Oqra develop as you shoot, and with Keep RAW Original on you can develop a RAW shot again from the album. That edit changes only that photo.

Does it shoot video, or just photos?

Both. Alongside photos and Live Photos, Oqra records film clips up to 30 seconds long (1920×1440, 24 fps), developed through the same preset.

Do my photos stay private?

Yes — everything develops on your phone. No account, no servers, nothing uploaded. See the privacy policy.

What iPhone do I need — and do I need a Pro?

iPhone 13 or newer, on iOS 18 or later. You don't need a Pro: Pro models capture in Apple ProRAW, every other iPhone captures full-resolution DNG, and Oqra develops both. ProRAW gives the engine the most to work with, so results can vary a little with what your camera captures — but a non-Pro iPhone is fully supported.
